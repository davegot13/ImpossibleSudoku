# Impossible Sudoku Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Impossible Sudoku, including its App Clip, does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. It makes no network connections. Your games, scores, personal bests, and settings are saved only on your device and are removed when you delete the app. When you move from the App Clip to the full app, nothing is transferred off your device.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
